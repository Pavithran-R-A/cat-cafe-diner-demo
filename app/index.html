How to Play

Miso is counting on you!

🐱 Guests queue at the garden door and sit at a free table. They read the menu, then raise a paw with a picture bubble of what they want.

👆 Click the table (or press its number 1–6) and Miso runs over to take the order. Chef Mochi cooks it in the kitchen — watch the pot's progress ring!

🍽 When the dish lands on the pass counter, press C or Space (or click the pass) so Miso grabs it — he can carry 2 dishes. Then click the glowing table to serve.

🪙 After eating, the guest leaves coins and an empty plate. Click the table to collect — a table is not free until you collect!

⏳ The 5 hearts above each guest drain as patience falls — happy pink, then annoyed orange, then red and steaming at 1 heart. Angry guests leave with no tip. Serve fast to build a combo, and watch for Rush Hour (+25% tips)!

🔗 Chains: repeat the same action back-to-back — two orders, two deliveries, or two bussed tables in a row — to climb the chain meter (×2, ×3, ×4…). A different action, or a guest's hearts running completely out (a walk-out), resets it.

⭐ Each day shows an Expert target in the top bar. Hit it (score + a ×4 chain + no walk-outs) for Expert honours on the day-end breakdown.

🐱 Every guest is a named Regular with a favourite dish and drink — check the Regulars Album. Serving a favourite grows friendship hearts.

🎯 Reach the day's money goal before the clock hits 9pm. Finish all 6 days to unlock endless Master Mode.
